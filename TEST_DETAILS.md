# Test Details – First Choice Transportation

Copy the text below into the **What to Test?** field in App Store Connect TestFlight.

## For TestFlight (paste this)

**DRIVER:**
• Log in with credentials provided by admin
• Start a shift (location must be enabled – test with location off to see blocking message)
• Check Location status shows Enabled/Disabled and Tracking Active/Inactive
• During shift: verify route tracking records your route
• Complete final dropoff to end shift (requires base location in Profile)
• Confirm Summary tab shows only your own shifts and hours

**ADMIN:**
• Log in as admin
• Create a new driver (full name, email, temporary password) – share credentials with driver
• Open Weekly Hours to see all drivers’ verified hours, shift count, and flagged count
• Tap a driver to view detail; tap a shift to view route and dropoff info

**GENERAL:**
• Test on a real device with GPS enabled
• Report any crashes, freezes, or incorrect data
